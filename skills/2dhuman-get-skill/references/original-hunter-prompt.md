# 猎手｜2D 横版像素角色提示词

## 设计依据与补充
- 文档依据：西式奇幻、偏暗真实色调；猎手自幼随师，在森林狩猎，曾击退追赶平民的暴徒；武器为弓，具有箭矢管理与灵活移动的职业特色。
- 本次选用：朴素旅行装束与榆木短弓，不画高阶防具。沿用低装备阶段视觉，不把正篇与 Demo 的相遇剧情拼成同一事件。
- 原创外形提案（非文档定案）：约28岁的精瘦男性，深栗短发、晒深肤色、警觉而克制；苔绿短上衣、褐灰裤、旧靴、磨损皮革箭袋。衣物有少量修补，无华丽装饰。
- 用户本次要求优先：2D 横版侧视，而非 GDD 原有等距俯视。
- 交付：一张单角色全身像素设计图，不是三视图、动画帧表或已经验证可直接导入游戏的精灵素材。

## 实际生图提示词

Create one polished 2D SIDE-SCROLLING PIXEL ART character illustration of a human HUNTER for a grounded, dark Western fantasy game. One character only, full body from hair to boot soles, facing RIGHT in a clean side-view gameplay silhouette, with only a slight torso turn to reveal the equipment. Orthographic side elevation, camera at torso height, NOT isometric, NOT top-down. Landscape canvas, single centered figure occupying about 80 percent of canvas height, generous clear space around the complete bow and feet.

Character story translated into visible design: a forest hunter trained by a mentor since childhood, now a vigilant protector of fleeing civilians after a kingdom's collapse. He is a lean, wiry adult man around 28, weathered sun-tanned skin, short practical dark chestnut hair, alert restrained expression, grounded human proportions around 6 heads tall, not chibi. His demeanor is resourceful and protective rather than sinister or aristocratic. Plain faded moss-green coarse-linen thigh-length tunic with short split hem for running, rolled sleeves and bare hands, muted brown-gray trousers, worn simple brown boots, narrow utilitarian belt, small practical belt pouch. A few readable repair patches and worn hems tell a life spent outdoors. NO armor, NO helmet, NO elaborate cape, NO luxurious decoration. A weathered brown leather quiver rides diagonally high on his back, several pale-feathered arrow ends visible above the shoulder. He carries a modest curved ELM SHORTBOW with a clearly visible taut single bowstring in his forward left hand, held low and ready, with the bow silhouette separated from his torso and legs. His right hand rests near the belt, relaxed but ready. Both boots planted on the same horizontal baseline, a subtly staggered balanced idle stance with slightly bent knees, ready to move quickly. Keep hands, bow, quiver and body anatomically and mechanically coherent. No drawn arrow, no magical effects.

Authentic carefully hand-placed pixel-art aesthetic: design as if on a low-resolution 320 by 224 pixel canvas and enlarged with nearest-neighbor scaling. Clearly visible consistent square pixel grid, crisp stepped edges, deliberate economical pixel clusters, restrained selective dark outlines, limited palette of roughly 24 colors, 3-tone material shading, small controlled highlights, no tiny noisy surface detail. Muted moss green, earth brown, charcoal, desaturated tan, warm pale arrow feathers. Strong readable silhouette and separated limbs suitable for a side-scrolling game. Subtle upper-left pixel lighting. A uniform flat light warm-gray background provides strong contrast against the hunter; only a tiny hard-edged pixel contact shadow beneath the boots, no environment or scenery. Beautiful professional indie-game sprite concept, not a pixelation filter over a painting. No smooth gradients, no antialiasing, no blur, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render, no vector art, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no frame, no duplicate character, no sprite sheet, no extra limbs, no cropped weapons or feet.
